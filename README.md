# 🌿 Lamina

**Lamina** is a plant identification application designed to help users discover and learn about the plants around them.

The name *Lamina* comes from the botanical term for the flat, expanded part of a leaf, commonly known as the **leaf blade**. This part plays an important role in plant identification, as its shape, structure, and other visible characteristics provide valuable clues for recognizing and classifying plant species.

## 🌱 About the Project

Lamina aims to make plant identification simple, accessible, and educational, encouraging users to explore and better understand the natural world.
